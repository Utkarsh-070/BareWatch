# Privacy Policy

BareWatch stores only the extension preferences selected by the user, such as whether to hide the YouTube home feed, Shorts, comments, or related videos. These preferences are stored locally in Chrome/Edge using `chrome.storage.local`.

BareWatch does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, browsing history, video URLs, search queries, YouTube account information, or page content. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts, or external server.

BareWatch runs only on YouTube pages so it can hide the interface sections selected by the user.

Removing BareWatch through the browser removes its locally stored extension data according to the browser's extension-data behavior. Users can reset preferences at any time from the BareWatch settings page.
